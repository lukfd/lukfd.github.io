## Intro

I have always been intrested in creating and designing solutions. The definition of startup for me might be different than yours or YC which they think that is a businness that can grow very fast and generate millions in revenue.

For me it all starts from an idea or a solution and building it. Some say that the idea is not important but the execution and the team (especially the founder) are the the most important aspects of a successful startup.

This page is just a collection of information I gather while living in St. Paul Minnesota and exploring the start up scene around here.

## Near St. Thomas

As a student at the University of St. Thomas, located in St. Paul, I participated two time at GAlpha which is organized by both the university and Gener8tor. GAlpha is for who is starting from scratch.

Recently the AI club has also organized a hackathon.

For St. Thomas students and alumni, it is possible to use St Thomas Connect.

## Accelerators and other programs

### Gener8tor

As we said before Gener8tor is the organization behind GAlpha. They also organize GBeta for startup that have already have launched a product and might already have some customers.

### Beta

I am not too familira with Beta, but I have been to a Showcase event in which their startup were pitching in front of investors.

### Tangle Rabbit

I have heard about Tangle Rabbit at the 2026 MinneBar where Beth Elliot was giving a talk. Some of the notes I took during the talk are:

1. Who might you become if you look beyond your resume?
2. What offer feels exciting to your customers? Example: I am going to help parents manage education. What does that mean? At what scale? How do you find customers?
3. What parts of your business are energizing? What parts are draining?

## Startup Events

### TC Startup Week

An event every year around September or October called TC Startup Week. It is a week long event with many sessions and activities around the startup theme.
