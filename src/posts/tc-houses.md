I have taken some photos of homes and houses around the twins cities. Here are the ones I liked the most.

### Number 6 and 7 (sixseven!) - Prospect Park

![Prospect Park Home 1](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_1351.jpg)
![Prospect Park Home 2](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_1353.jpg)

These homes are both in the Prospect Park of Saint Paul. I love the entire neigborhood because it's all covered by trees and right next to the Prospect Park "witch" tower that overlooks at Minneapolis. The tower itself sits on one of the highest peaks of the Twins Cities.


### Number 5 - Ford Parkway

![Ford Parkway Home](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_1539.jpg)

This is a beatiful home in the Ford Parkway area of Saint Paul in the Highland Park neighborhood. I love how it has a beatiful tower, in a way, similar to the witch tower in Prospect park. The windows are tall and the walls white, and a disproportinate straight cimney.


### Number 4 - The Pencil

![Pencil House](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_1705.jpg)

The house itself is nothing fancy, but the Pencil gives everything a reason to exists. In Chicago they say, we have the Bean... well we say we have the Pencil. Be aware that the Pencil might be gone in few years of sharpening.


### Number 3 - Lake of the Isles

![Lake of the Isles Home](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_1707.jpg)

This photo I took does not do enough justice, but right next to the Pencil house in the Lake of the Isles area, there is this beautiful home that looks like just some spanish people built it. The yellow bricks and the little balcony are so unique.

### Number 2 - University Club Saint Paul

![University Club Saint Paul](/photos/blog/tc-houses/IMG_9763.jpg)

The University Club building in Saint Paul is magnificent. It secretly keeps all its beauty inside where you can find a magastic globe and a wonderful view of Saint Paul. The wooden walls and the carpets make the entire building 


### Number 1 - The Stevens' Home

![Marshall Home](/photos/blog/tc-houses/DSC06996.jpg)
